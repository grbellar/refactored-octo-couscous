Question

What is the capital of France?





Explanation

Paris is indeed the capital city of France, serving as its political and cultural center since the Middle Ages. The city is home to iconic landmarks like the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre Museum, and Notre-Dame Cathedral. With a population of over 2 million people in the city proper, Paris is also France's largest metropolitan area. The city's rich history, architecture, cuisine, and fashion have made it one of the world's most visited tourist destinations.
