Our Mission

PerfusionBoardPrep is dedicated to helping recent cardiovascular perfusion graduates and professionals prepare for board certification exams. We offer a comprehensive set of study materials designed to build the knowledge and confidence required to succeed in both the Basic Science and Clinical Application portions of the certification exam.

How this works

Prepare with six practice exams and over 500 quizzes.

Each exam consists of 100 questions and has a 2 hour time limit—exactly half the length of the real board exam.

After completing each practice exam, you'll receive a detailed breakdown of your performance across 11 key categories. This mirrors the structure of the real board exam.

Quizzes offer immediate feedback and detailed answer explanations.

Textbook sources are provided for further studying. Most quizzes have 10 questions and allow you to go at your own pace. You'll have 8 hours to review each quiz once started.
